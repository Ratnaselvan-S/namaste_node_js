put vs patch HW

        -> PUT replaces the entire resource with the new data you send. If some fields are missing, they may be overwritten or removed.

        -> PATCH updates only the specific fields you provide, leaving the rest unchanged.

        -> Use PUT when you want full replacement; use PATCH for partial modification.

        -> PUT is generally idempotent (same request → same result), while PATCH may or may not be.
